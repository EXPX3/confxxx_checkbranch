\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Dataset, Splits, and Spatial Resolution}
We use the OccuFly scene split: 01--05 for training, 06--07 for validation, and 08--09 for testing, at 30, 40, and 50~m \cite{gross2026occufly}. It contains 14,804/1,965/3,842 depth targets. RGB is resized to $384\times384$; valid camera-axis depth is finite and within $[0.001,80]$~m. The temporal diagnostic excludes sequence boundaries and evaluates 3,830 targets, so it is not ranked as a full-test monocular result.

SSC uses a $192\times128\times128$ grid with 0.5-m voxels over $[-48,48]\times[-32,32]\times[0,64]$~m. Labels comprise empty, 21 occupied classes, and ignored label 255. All reported SSC artifacts match the canonical 22-class ground-truth support and valid-voxel total; because the multiview records lack ordered manifest hashes, we claim aggregate consistency rather than exact target-identity equivalence.

\subsection{Training and Checkpoint Selection}
MDE trains only the named head with AdamW and masked valid-depth supervision; single-run image comparisons use seed~0, 20 epochs, and validation RMSE selection. Table~\ref{tab:mde} retains audited test records; unavailable metrics remain blank, and unfinished controls are excluded.

Monocular VoxDet uses AdamW with main/depth learning rates $2\times10^{-4}/10^{-6}$ and seeds 42--44. The final multiview model trains context and VoxDet at $10^{-4}$ while DINOv3, MVSFormer++, and view fusion remain frozen. Its three 20-epoch runs use seeds 0--2, one-epoch warm-up, cosine decay, weight decay $10^{-4}$, gradient clipping at five, and global batch eight on eight H100 GPUs. Checkpoints are validation-selected without test feedback. The historical V-JEPA seed-42 record ends at epoch~8; consequently, cross-configuration differences are descriptive rather than equal-budget factorial effects.

\subsection{Metrics and Audit}
Depth metrics are accumulated over valid pixels. $\delta_q$ is the fraction within a multiplicative factor $1.25^q$ (strict to tolerant); AbsRel measures relative error, MAE typical metric error, and RMSE emphasizes large metric errors. SILog is the spread of log-depth residuals after removing their mean, hence it penalizes spatially varying multiplicative error but discounts one global log-scale offset. For SSC, SC IoU merges all occupied classes and measures geometry; precision/recall separate false occupancy from missed occupancy. Semantic mIoU is the fixed macro average over 21 occupied classes, so every class has equal weight; empty and ignored voxels are excluded and an absent class contributes zero. Figures use N/A only to distinguish absent support from zero true positives.

Rows reported as mean$\pm$SD use three independent runs. MDE-5 is the three-run mean in Table~\ref{tab:film-aggregate}; the other local rows in Table~\ref{tab:mde} are single validation-selected runs, and MDE-11 is a published point estimate. SSC-3--SSC-5 are three-run means; SSC-1, SSC-2, and O5 are single runs, while external rows are published point estimates. Saved confusion marginals reproduce the SSC metrics. Altitude-wise MDE evaluates every one of the 3,842 UIDs once; class support denotes the test, not training, distribution.

Dense decoding and camera conditioning improve both backbones (Table~\ref{tab:mde}). Relative to their linear probes, FiLM-DPT reduces V-JEPA~2.1 RMSE from 7.808 to 4.910~m and DINOv3 RMSE from 6.097 to 4.362~m. The benefit of the heavier frozen DINOv3 model is prediction quality, not demonstrated efficiency: with a trained FiLM-DPT head it exceeds the fine-tuned DepthAnything2--OccuFly reference in RMSE, MAE, AbsRel, and $\delta_1$, but DepthAnything2 retains substantially lower SILog (0.112 vs. 0.161). The contracts and capacities differ, and no DepthAnything3--OccuFly result is available, so we do not claim general model superiority.

Lower SILog requires reducing spatial variation in multiplicative error, not merely correcting one global scale. Relevant controlled directions are a log-depth/SILog-aware objective, multiscale and boundary supervision, and validation selection that includes SILog while retaining metric-error constraints. Such changes require new validation-selected runs; the present test table cannot establish their effect. Figure~\ref{fig:backbone_feature_diagnostics} illustrates feature/readout structure but its independently fitted PCA colors are not quantitative evidence.

Altitude supplies an absolute scene-scale cue, while normalized intrinsics specify how pixels map to camera rays. Their joint FiLM modulation gives the strongest three-seed V-JEPA result (Table~\ref{tab:film-aggregate}); altitude alone helps, whereas intrinsics alone is below unconditioned DPT, so the evidence supports their learned combination rather than either metadata source universally.

MDE-3 uses two-source hypothesis warping and 3D convolution; Fig.~\ref{fig:dta_raft_compact} shows the preceding RAFT alignment. MVSFormer++ instead uses one target and four sources in a coarse-to-fine inverse-depth cascade with cross-view attention and transformer cost regularization \cite{cao2024mvsformerpp}. Under the retained matched single-run contract, D3-H+ improves MVSFormer++ RMSE from 6.390 to 4.611~m relative to VJ-G. Only the frozen backbone family is intentionally changed, but feature widths, pretraining, adapter parameters, and selected epochs differ. Compatibility of DINOv3 spatial features with correspondence is therefore a plausible explanation, not an identified cause; repeated runs and controlled feature adapters are needed.

FoundationSSC contributes decoupled semantic/geometry pathways, complementary LSS--VoxFormer lifting, and axis-aware fusion; VoxDet contributes separate semantic/regression voxel streams and offset-guided semantic aggregation. Their combination is structurally complementary: the former constructs and fuses the 3D evidence, while the latter decodes it with boundary-aware aggregation. SSC-3--SSC-5 outperform the local controls in Table~\ref{tab:ssc}, but loss and optimization also change, so the results support the complete hybrids rather than a numerical contribution from either module alone.

O5 replaces MVSFormer++ with mask-aware GT metric depth throughout training and inference. GT depth conditions the 2D context branch and supplies the Gaussian posterior for LSS lifting, proposals, and VoxFormer; GT semantics never enter inference. Its single validation-selected run reaches 54.66\%/8.33\% SC IoU/mIoU. This is an empirical privileged diagnostic, not a theoretical maximum: the large residual to 100\% shows limits in context, fusion, decoding, taxonomy, and long-tail supervision, while better deployable depth can only be expected to move toward---not necessarily reach---O5.

Altitude-wise results reinforce this distinction. MDE-8 and all SSC rows are weakest at 40~m, consistent with geometry contributing to completion. Yet MDE is strongest at 30~m while SSC-5 is strongest at 50~m, showing that depth alone does not determine SSC. O5 improves both SSC metrics at every altitude, but as a single run it provides headroom rather than a variance-controlled altitude effect.

\subsection{Repeated-Run Multiview Result}
SSC-5 obtains $49.3278\pm1.1704\%$ SC IoU and $4.8025\pm0.3648\%$ mIoU. All three seeds exceed the historical multiview control in compatible binary SC IoU, with a mean difference of 5.96 points. The control lacks matched seeds and compatible semantic marginals, so this establishes repeatability of the complete treatment, not paired significance or an isolated MVS effect.

Empty occupies 85.14\% of valid test voxels, and building 86.21\% of occupied support (Fig.~\ref{fig:longtail}). SSC-5 reaches $46.13\pm1.60\%$ building IoU but at most $11.11\pm0.86\%$ on the listed remaining classes. Person, bicycle, and cable have positive support yet zero true positives in all runs; water, cable tower, and crane have no test support and are marked N/A. This explains the gap between completion and macro semantics but does not identify frequency as the sole cause: visibility, geometry, voxel size, annotation, and confusion remain possible factors, and training-support histograms are unavailable.
