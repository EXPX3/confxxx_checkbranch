\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Repeated-Run Evaluation of Multiview SSC}

We test whether the DINOv3--MVSFormer++--VoxDet result persists across
initialization and sampling randomness while holding the geometry provider
fixed.  Seeds 0, 1, and 2 use the same dataset split, frozen native
MVSFormer++ checkpoint, five-view input construction, optimizer, learning-rate
schedule, loss weights, and validation-only checkpoint-selection rule.  Model
and sampler seeds are aligned within each run.  The selected epochs are 13,
12, and 11, respectively.  We report the arithmetic mean and sample standard
deviation with Bessel's correction ($n-1$, $n=3$).

Table~\ref{tab:multiview-ssc-seeds} reports binary scene-completion (SC) IoU and three
semantic averaging conventions.  GT-present mIoU averages the 18 occupied
classes represented in test ground truth.  Fixed-21 mIoU zero-fills the three
GT-absent occupied classes and always divides by 21; we use it as the primary
semantic result because its denominator is prediction-independent.
Union-present mIoU is retained only for compatibility with the historical MVS
control, and uses 19, 19, and 20 classes for seeds 0, 1, and 2.

All three runs exceed the single historical DINOv3--MVSFormer++ FoundationSSC
control in the two directly comparable fields: the control reports 43.3670\%
SC IoU and 4.4728\% union-present mIoU.  Thus, the observed direction persists
over the three tested runs. This is not a paired significance result:
the control lacks repeated seeds and its historical artifact does not retain
sufficient marginals for a fixed-21 rescore.

\subsection{Class-Wise Stability}

Table~\ref{tab:multiview-ssc-classes} gives all occupied-class IoUs. Person, bicycle,
and cable are GT-present failures with zero IoU for every seed.  Water, cable
tower, and crane have no test GT support, so their IoUs are undefined for a
GT-conditioned class analysis and are shown as dashes rather than semantic
successes.  Performance gains are most stable for building, tree, vehicle,
parking lot, grass, and the transport-surface classes, while rare classes
remain unresolved.
