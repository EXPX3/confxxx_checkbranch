\begin{abstract}
Aerial 3D perception must recover metric geometry and semantic occupancy despite altitude-dependent scale, oblique viewpoints, and limited onboard sensing. We study the transfer of frozen V-JEPA~2.1 image--video representations and DINOv3 image representations to monocular depth estimation (MDE) and semantic scene completion (SSC) on OccuFly. Controlled heads range from linear probes to multiscale DPT decoding, camera-conditioned FiLM, temporal aggregation, and calibrated multiview geometry. The strongest completed MDE model, DINOv3 with FiLM-DPT, attains 4.358~m test RMSE. For SSC, combining frozen DINOv3 context, frozen MVSFormer++ geometry, and VoxDet decoding yields $49.33\pm1.17\%$ scene-completion IoU and $4.80\pm0.36\%$ semantic mIoU across three independent runs. Class-wise analysis shows that the remaining error is concentrated in sparsely represented categories, separating gains in occupied-space recovery from unresolved long-tail semantics.\end{abstract}
